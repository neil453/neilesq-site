Fit Body Nation Restored to Possession After Contested Commercial Lockout Trial

Neil L. Postrygacz obtained an order restoring Fit Body Nation LLC and Danny White to possession of their Manhattan commercial premises after a contested illegal lockout trial.

Discuss a Commercial Dispute Commercial Litigation Practice

The Result

The court found that Fit Body Nation had the right to occupy the premises and ordered restoration of possession. The ruling followed a contested trial in which the opposing side challenged the client's credibility and disputed his right to remain in the space.

The firm entered the matter after the proceeding was already underway, at a point when the court had indicated that the client should have counsel. From there, the focus was on building a clear evidentiary record, identifying the contradictions that mattered, and presenting the testimony and documents in a way that addressed the central factual disputes directly.

Trial work matters when credibility is attacked.

Focused witness examination, careful use of the documentary record and disciplined presentation of the facts can change the direction of a case when the dispute turns on competing versions of what happened.

Credibility Was Put at the Center of the Case

The opposing side placed Danny White's credibility squarely in issue. The response was not rhetoric. It was evidence. Through focused witness examination and the documentary record, the trial presentation tested the competing accounts and established the factual basis for Fit Body Nation's right to possession.

That approach is the same one the firm brings to commercial litigation more broadly. Whether the forum is a state court, federal court, arbitration or an emergency proceeding, the objective is to identify what actually decides the dispute, develop the proof around those issues and be prepared to try the case when settlement is not a viable solution.

Commercial Litigation Is Often Won in the Details

Business disputes frequently turn on credibility, contracts, communications, course of performance and the quality of the evidentiary record. Strong advocacy requires more than filing papers. It requires understanding the record well enough to examine witnesses effectively, expose inconsistencies and connect the evidence to the relief the client needs.

For Fit Body Nation, that meant pursuing restoration after a commercial lockout had interrupted the business. The court ordered possession restored, and the matter is continuing into the damages phase, including briefing concerning damages available under New York law.

Protecting Clients When Settlement Is Not Enough

Neil L. Postrygacz represents businesses, executives and entrepreneurs in consequential disputes. The goal is to resolve matters efficiently when a fair business solution is available. When it is not, the firm prepares the case to be won through motion practice, hearings, witness examination and trial.
