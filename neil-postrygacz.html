Commercial Litigation and Strategic Advocacy

Neil L. Postrygacz represents businesses, executives, entrepreneurs and investors in significant commercial disputes. His practice focuses on identifying the legal and factual issues that matter most, protecting leverage and pursuing the client's business objective through negotiation, motion practice, discovery, hearings and trial.

Matters are handled throughout the United States in federal and state courts, arbitration and mediation, with local counsel involved where required. The representation is direct and senior-level, with strategy and advocacy handled by the attorney responsible for the matter.

Neil also serves as general counsel to a telecommunications company, advising on contracts, commercial transactions, risk, disputes and day-to-day legal issues. That general counsel role brings a business-side perspective to both litigation strategy and transactional work.

Approach

Resolve what can be resolved efficiently. When a fair resolution is not available, prepare the record, develop the evidence and litigate the dispute through the forum and stage required to protect the client's interests.

Practice Focus

Complex Commercial Litigation Contract, ownership, fiduciary, fraud, business tort, investment and other consequential business disputes.
Cross-Border Discovery 28 U.S.C. § 1782 applications, subpoenas and contested discovery for use in qualifying foreign proceedings.
Technology and Emerging Industries Disputes involving digital assets, telecommunications, confidential technology, trade secrets and evolving business models.
Arbitration and Mediation Commercial disputes handled in arbitral proceedings and mediation as well as state and federal courts.
